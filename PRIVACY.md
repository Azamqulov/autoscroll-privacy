# Auto Scroll — Maxfiylik siyosati / Privacy Policy

_Oxirgi yangilanish / Last updated: 2026-11-22_

## O'zbekcha

**Auto Scroll** (Instagram Reels va YouTube Shorts uchun avtomatik scroll kengaytmasi) foydalanuvchi ma'lumotlarini yig'maydi, sotmaydi va uchinchi tomonlarga bermaydi.

### Qanday ma'lumotlar saqlanadi?
Quyidagi ma'lumotlar **faqat sizning brauzeringizda** (`chrome.storage` va IndexedDB) saqlanadi va hech qachon serverga yuborilmaydi:
- kengaytma sozlamalari (kutish vaqti, yoqilgan platformalar, audio effektlar, «Fonda ishlash» va boshqalar);
- shaxsiy statistika: ko'rilgan videolar soni, tomosha vaqti, avtomatik o'tishlar soni.

Statistikani Dashboard sahifasidan istalgan vaqt eksport qilish yoki o'chirish mumkin. Kengaytmani o'chirib tashlasangiz, bu ma'lumotlar ham o'chadi.

### Kengaytma nimalarni tekshiradi?
- Faqat `instagram.com` va `youtube.com` sahifalarida videoni aniqlaydi va keyingisiga o'tadi.
- «Faqat musiqali reelslar» rejimida reelsning ovozi **qurilmaning o'zida** tahlil qilinadi (nutq yoki musiqa ekanini aniqlash uchun). Ovoz yozib olinmaydi, saqlanmaydi va hech qayerga yuborilmaydi.
- «Reklama / Sponsored» belgisi sahifadagi matn orqali qurilmada tekshiriladi.

### Nimalar qilinmaydi?
- Shaxsiy ma'lumot (ism, email, joylashuv va h.k.) so'ralmaydi va yig'ilmaydi.
- Analitika, reklama yoki kuzatuv vositalari yo'q.
- Tashqi serverlarga so'rov yuborilmaydi, masofadan kod yuklanmaydi.

### Ruxsatlar
- `storage` — sozlamalar va statistikani brauzeringizda saqlash uchun.
- `instagram.com`, `youtube.com` sahifalariga kirish — videoni aniqlash, keyingisiga o'tish va audio effektlarni qo'llash uchun.

### Mustaqillik
Auto Scroll mustaqil kengaytma bo'lib, Instagram, Meta, YouTube yoki Google bilan bog'liq emas va ular tomonidan tasdiqlanmagan.

## English

**Auto Scroll** (an auto-scroll extension for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts) does not collect, sell or share any user data.

### What is stored?
The following is stored **only in your own browser** (`chrome.storage` and IndexedDB) and is never sent to any server:
- extension settings (delay, enabled platforms, audio effects, "Background play", etc.);
- local usage statistics: videos watched, watch time, number of automatic transitions.

You can export or delete the statistics at any time from the Dashboard page. Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

### What does the extension inspect?
- It works only on `instagram.com` and `youtube.com` pages, where it detects the current video and moves to the next one.
- In "Music only" mode the reel's audio is analysed **locally on your device** to tell speech from music. Audio is never recorded, stored or transmitted.
- "Sponsored" labels are detected locally from the page text.

### What it does not do
- No personal data (name, email, location, etc.) is requested or collected.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- No requests to external servers and no remote code.

### Permissions
- `storage` — to keep settings and statistics in your browser.
- Access to `instagram.com` and `youtube.com` — to detect videos, advance to the next one and apply audio effects.

### Independence
Auto Scroll is an independent extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram, Meta, YouTube or Google.
